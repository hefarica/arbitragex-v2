Seven concrete new strategy types NOT in the 264 catalog (which I verified contains none of these as such — its CEX-DEX/derivatives/lending families have no LVR-band, auction-bidding, fee-vol, shielded-venue, or certified-adversarial entries), each with topology and math:

1. LVR-BAND CONVERGENCE TRIGGER (stale-state execution gate). Topology: OrthogonalEquilibrium, 2-leg non-cyclic (DEX pool vs CEX/external mid). Math: maintain external reference price S_t (CEX mid or aggregator); pool executable price P_pool(f,gas); execute iff |S_t − P_pool| > band B(f;v) + gas + bidders'-cut, where B is the no-arbitrage band half-width from the adverse-selection rate A(f;v) = (v/8)·(1+√(2λ/v)·f)^{-1} (arXiv:2606.21769), with per-block correction (arXiv:2505.05113) and pool-pair bounded-liquidity extension (arXiv:2507.02027). Size: Kelly fraction of edge/variance with delay penalty (arXiv:1907.08771). This upgrades every CEX-DEX and cross-venue strategy from fixed min_profit thresholds to a volatility-adaptive optimal-stopping surface; it also encodes WHY 100ms blocks cut LVR 20-70% (arXiv:2404.05803). Production evidence: stale state is the dominant routing-shortfall driver (arXiv:2607.20762).

2. PPO AUCTION-BIDDING POLICY LAYER (execution overlay, not a detector). Topology: applies to ANY Execution_Class=DETERMINISTIC_EXECUTABLE opportunity; the strategy IS the bidding policy. Math: state x_t = route features (protocol types, length, historical frequency) + windowed win rates + aggregated recent opponent bids; action b_t ∈ [0,1] bribe fraction of opportunity value v_t; reward r = π/(v+ε) − λ𝕀{lost} − α(b−b̃)₊ (b̃ = current top bid); PPO with clipped surrogate, Gaussian/Beta policy head, GAE; history-conditioning H=10 windows × K=5 samples for partial observability. Benchmarks: MPC 80.93% vs 56.54% incumbent, 143% of incumbent profit on 223,356 real Polygon Atlas auctions (arXiv:2510.14642). Affiliated-value structure says second-price logic dominates when applicable (arXiv:2603.16333). This is op_31 (PPO) promoted from an operator to a LEARNED POLICY with production numbers attached.

3. JIT-LP LVR-IMBALANCE CAPTURE. Topology: OrthogonalEquilibrium (concentrated-range LP position for 1 block, not a swap cycle). Math: enter a narrow Uniswap-v3/v4 range around detected imminent toxic flow (state-triggered surface already in the catalog: 31 DEX_STATE strategies); expected per-block fee income f·ν(f) with ν(f)=ν₀e^{−αf} must exceed the LVR increment (σ²/8)·V_range·Δt_block + gas; regime gate via Stein thresholds before rebalancing (RAmmStein, arXiv:2602.19419 — deep RL beats greedy and cuts gas); abort condition when flow-toxicity estimate (true-aggressor VPIN, arXiv:2606.04217) says the next trader is noise, not informed. Production evidence: JIT bots are a documented equilibrium player in the Uniswap-v3 MFG Stackelberg model (arXiv:2404.09090) and a named distorter of stylized facts (arXiv:2510.22834).

4. FEE-IMPLIED-VOLATILITY STAT ARB (two pools, one pair). Topology: HolonomicLoopResolution across the FEE STATE of two same-pair pools (e.g., WETH-USDC 5bps vs 30bps) — a loop through (pool1, fee-level-1) → (pool2, fee-level-2) → back, where the "asset" traded is volatility-of-flow. Math: extract fee-implied vol from pool observables (arXiv:2509.23222); respect the identification bound — structural vol not identified from pool data alone (arXiv:2608.13340) — so trade the SPREAD of fee-implied vols between two pools (common structural vol cancels); model spread as OU with online Welford moments (existing op_10); entry at k·σ_OU, exit at k/4; size by fractional Kelly. Hedge leg: perp-installment-option replication of LVR exposure (arXiv:2508.02971) if the venue offers options/intents.

5. BOUNDED-LIQUIDITY CROSS-ROLLUP NON-ATOMIC ARB. Topology: CROSS_CHAIN (30 strategies exist) but with new math: per-leg depth-aware sizing from the two-imperfectly-liquid-markets LVR extension (arXiv:2507.02027) instead of fixed slippage caps, plus an inclusion-timing game on BOTH rollups (queueing model, arXiv:2401.08302; adversarial queues arXiv:2302.01663). Evidence of surface: >500,000 quantified unexploited cross-rollup opportunities (arXiv:2406.02172); cross-chain trigger surface via source-chain events discovered at $5.27M scale (arXiv:2511.15245). Portfolio layer: ergodic growth-rate maximization across the opportunity stream (criterion of arXiv:2606.21769 — per-strategy optimal control independent of wealth/risk-aversion, so sizing collapses to a global fractional-Kelly scalar).

6. CERTIFIED-ADVERSARIAL SURFACE (sandwich/backrun with verified bounds + toxicity precision filter). Topology: DEX_STATE-triggered HolonomicLoopResolution (front-run → victim → back-run legs). Math: use the Lean-CERTIFIED optimal front/back sizing for CPMMs (optimality machine-checked, arXiv:2510.14480) rather than heuristic sizing; gate by true-aggressor toxicity (the swap initiator IS the aggressor label on-chain — the exact data advantage of arXiv:2606.04217) and by pool-fee profitability floor (fees can make oracle-manipulation sandwiches unprofitable, arXiv:2602.12104); precision-first filtering because most flagged sandwiches on private-mempool venues are false positives (arXiv:2601.19570). Ethics/policy gate applies (project MEV-ethics gate) — the math is certified either way and doubles as a DEFENSE detector (A2MM-style protection sizing).

7. SHIELDED-VENUE SIZE-ESCALATION (privacy-noise exploitation). Topology: OrthogonalEquilibrium against DP-noised/batched venues (Penumbra-style, or future v4 hooks adding noise). Math: in the Bayesian-AMM Kyle equilibrium with privacy noise σₑ: λ = σᵥ/(2√(σᵤ²+σₑ²)), so informed size β = √(σᵤ²+σₑ²)/σᵥ GROWS with privacy noise while price impact λ FALLS (λβ = 1/2 invariant); the LP subsidy |πM| = σᵥσₑ²/(2√(σᵤ²+σₑ²)) is your expected edge transferred from the pool (arXiv:2605.15746). Practical rule: route informed-size DISPROPORTIONATELY to venues with flow-noise/batching, capped by the venue's break-even fee schedule; on batched (Penumbra-type) venues the effect collapses to σᵤ√τ scaling — so monitor which venues actually add noise.

8. (Bonus) LP-SIDE UTILITY-INDIFFERENCE PRICING / FEE-ARBITRAGE BETWEEN LP-ING AND REBALANCING. Topology: OrthogonalEquilibrium. Math: with nonzero fee and continuous external prices LVR vanishes (arXiv:2502.01931) — so the LP-vs-rebalancing spread is a pure fee/fee-tier arbitrage; price IL exactly as a vanilla-option strip via Carr-Madan spanning in (price, liquidity) coordinates (arXiv:2603.01344) and pick the side (LP vs hedge-replicate) with cheaper implied cost. This is a derivatives-family strategy the 30-strategy derivatives block lacks.

What production actors do better than what the 264-strategy Excel encodes (the Excel is strong on taxonomy/topology — 264 families x 31 operators with discovery equations — but the state of the art adds these dimensions the catalog does not capture):

1. **Simulation as a competitive weapon, not just a checkpoint.** Leaders run REVM in-process with bytecode caching (cacache), account mocking, storage-slot mocking, and custom revert-encoding quoters — ~19ms for 100 quotes on a local node (~0.2ms/quote) and 90% fewer RPC calls vs naive eth_call ([hotpath.rs benchmarks](https://hotpath.rs/blog/revm-alloy-anvil-arbitrage)). The Excel's strategies assume reserves/slot0/ticks inputs; it does not encode a simulation-substrate strategy layer (cache warmth, state-diff preload from a local reth/erigon snapshot, `transact` vs `transact_commit` semantics, stateOverride-based adversarial tests). Searchers that control the simulation substrate win on latency, which per the EigenPhi 2025 data is the *only* thing that separates the ~6 profitable actors from everyone averaging $3/attack.

2. **Bidding as a first-class strategy variable (auction theory + ML).** In MEV-Boost/private OFAs the strategy is not "find spread, execute" but "choose bid b in a first-price all-pay-ish auction against unknown competitors." The literature formalizes this ([Price of MEV](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.13464v1), [Roughgarden post-MEV TFMs](https://timroughgarden.org/papers/eobp3.pdf)) and practitioners apply ML to historical auctions ([IACR 2023/1281](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1281), [RL for MEV on Polygon, arXiv 2510.14642](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14642v1)). The Excel has Kelly/op_24 Nash/op_31 PPO as operators but the strategy definitions bake in static `min_profit_usd`/EV thresholds — no winner's-curse adjustment, no bid-vs-inclusion-probability curve, no competitor-count state.

3. **Economic-calibration feedback loops.** The 2025 empirics (average sandwich profit ~$3; only six actors >$10k; arbitrage $3.37M/30d spread across a dominant minority) mean each cartridge's expected value depends on *relative* latency and equilibrium occupancy, not the raw spread. Production systems recalibrate per-strategy win rates and realized-vs-simulated deltas from live ledgers; the Excel is a static prior (the repo itself flags flat_prior/zero-calibration as blocker A1/A2/M6).

4. **Formal verification and invariant harnesses on the *bot's own* contracts.** Certora's 2025 open-sourcing ([blog](https://www.certora.com/blog/certora-goes-open-source)), Halmos turning Foundry suites into proofs ([a16z](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/implementing-stateful-invariant-testing-with-halmos/)), and Echidna's symexec mode ([writeup](https://gustavo-grieco.github.io/blog/echidna-symexec/)) make "profit>=0 after gas in all reachable states", "flash loan repaid", "no reentrancy into router" provable. The Excel has no FV/invariant dimension at all.

5. **Inventory-based cross-chain execution instead of bridge-riding routes.** The TU Munich ACM 2025 study shows vertically-integrated actors dominate cross-chain arb by pre-positioning inventory on both chains and skipping bridges ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3771566), [CoW practitioner view](https://cow.fi/learn/cross-chain-mev-how-bots-extract-value-when-you-move-between-chains)). The Excel's 16 NONATOMIC_BRIDGE_REQUIRED strategies route *through* bridges — the losing topology; the winning one is two independent same-chain executions plus lazy rebalancing, with liquidity-poisoning avoidance ([IMDEA](https://dspace.networks.imdea.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.12761/2037/CCW_Paper.pdf?sequence=1)).

6. **Orderflow access as a moat (MEV-Share/OFA/private RPC/TEE).** Winners integrate MEV-Share hint streams, OFAs, and builder relationships; 2025 work benchmarks the protection/rebate tradeoffs ([arXiv 2505.19708](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19708v1), [MEV-Share docs](https://docs.flashbots.net/flashbots-mev-share/introduction)). The Excel treats orderflow only as a *user-side* thing to trade against; it does not model participating in the OFA itself (capturing rebates, buying exclusive flow).

7. **Protocol-upgrade awareness as an alpha source.** Aave V4 Dutch-auction liquidations and SVR ($16.7M recaptured) change liquidation math mid-2025 ([Aave blog](https://aave.com/blog/aave-v4-liquidations)); v4 hooks change AMM geometry per-pool ([Uniswap security framework](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/security)); ePBS/FOCIL will change inclusion economics in ~Q3 2026 ([EIP-7732](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7732)). A static Excel cannot encode "strategy value is a function of protocol version"; production systems version their detectors per protocol release.

8. **Security telemetry as alpha AND defense**: real-time oracle-manipulation monitors ([Guardrail](https://www.guardrail.ai/common-attack-vectors/price-oracle-manipulation)), forensic replay ([Dedaub Cork post-mortem](https://dedaub.com/blog/the-11m-cork-protocol-hack-a-critical-lesson-in-uniswap-v4-hook-security/)), per-feed staleness windows ([Cyfrin](https://medium.com/cyfrin/chainlink-oracle-defi-attacks-93b6cb6541bf)) — the same detectors that protect a protocol also *are* the trigger condition for several arbitrage families (oracle-lag arb, liquidation windows).

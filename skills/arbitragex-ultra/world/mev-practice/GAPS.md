# What ArbitrageX is missing vs state of the art

Honest assessment of ArbitrageX vs the 2024-2026 state of the art (informed by the repo memory: SizeOptimizer is the live economic engine; 264 cartridges mostly return is_opp=false; Kelly is advisory pending calibration; route_metadata gaps exist):

1. NO ADVERSE-SELECTION/LVR OPERATOR (biggest gap). The 31-operator set has no concept of state staleness, no-arbitrage bands, or σ²V/8-type costs. Net-profit gates use static slippage caps, while the field's central execution result is that staleness dominates PnL (2.02 bps average shortfall on WETH-USDC, mostly stale-state-driven, arXiv:2607.20762). Concretely: a detector that finds positive Π_R(x) on one-block-old reserves is finding money that mostly is not there.

2. DETECTION-ORIENTED vs OPTIMIZATION-ORIENTED. 264 detectors with prose discovery equations vs one per-token marginal-price root-finding program that certifies optimal splits and runs 200x faster than generic solvers (arXiv:2502.08258). The catalog's per-strategy equations cannot express multi-pool split + gas tradeoffs (the G-FVO benchmark). This is plausibly a root cause of the observed "264 cartridges return is_opp=false" phenomenon: individually gated detectors on thin edges, instead of a global optimizer that aggregates thin edges into executable splits.

3. NO LEARNED AUCTION/BIDDING POLICY. Inclusion at MEV-relevant priority is a repeated auction with affiliated values against concentrated incumbents (11 searchers = 80% of $132B; 3 of 19 = 75% of $233.8M; exclusive builder ties — arXiv:2401.01622, 2507.13023). op_24 Nash and op_29 Shapley are static; nothing estimates win-probability curves or learns opponent models. The published PPO bidder (80.93% MPC, arXiv:2510.14642) is the template; currently there is no bid-policy surface at all.

4. NO EMPIRICAL ATTRIBUTION LOOP vs COMPUTED OPTIMA. The system has simulation gates and paper-shadow ledgers, but no shortfall accounting against SCO/FVO/G-FVO-style benchmarks, no replayed-trade comparison, no DiD-style evaluation of config changes (arXiv:2607.08525). Without it, "0 viable opportunities" cannot be decomposed into market-thinness vs detector-suboptimality vs staleness — which matches the repo's own memory note that the 0-opps state remained undiagnosed across branches.

5. ML PLACEMENT IS INVERTED vs EVIDENCE. The 31 operators include op_30 GNN Encoder (route prediction) and op_31 PPO, but published production evidence supports ML for BIDDING/REBALANCING/DETECTION (PPO bidder, RAmmStein, GasTrace 96.73%) and NO production GNN routing evidence — production routing cores are exact convex/combinatorial + simulation. GNN-as-route-predictor is the least-evidenced operator in the set; the exact solver (Newton/golden-section/convex root-finding) is the most-evidenced.

6. TOXICITY FILTERS UNVALIDATED ON-CHAIN. VPIN/flow-toxicity skills derive from CEX-style classifiers; on-chain ground truth shows inferred VPIN diverges substantially from true-aggressor VPIN (arXiv:2606.04217). The on-chain advantage (swaps are aggressor-labeled by construction) is not exploited; and sandwich detectors on private-mempool venues have a false-positive problem (arXiv:2601.19570) that the precision-first literature now handles explicitly.

7. NO VOLATILITY SURFACE/IDENTITY MATH. 30 derivatives strategies but no fee-implied volatility extraction (arXiv:2509.23222), no awareness of the non-identifiability bound (arXiv:2608.13340), no installment-option replication identities (arXiv:2508.02971), no Carr-Madan IL spanning (arXiv:2603.01344). Derivative detectors price payoffs without the replication identities that define their hedged edge.

8. TAIL RISK IS CONCEPTUAL, NOT PARAMETERIZED. EVT appears only implicitly (op_22 Monte Carlo). No GPD/POT parameterization of depeg/oracle-manipulation tails (arXiv:2608.09576 pattern, AUC 0.82 one-bar-ahead), no EVT-copula joint-extreme modeling for simultaneous multi-pool dislocations (arXiv:2605.10303), no per-chain asymmetry (ETH heavier tails than BTC, arXiv:2507.01983) in risk limits — relevant to the kill-switch thresholds (500ms divergence breakers are latency rules, not tail rules).

9. KELLY IS A FORMULA, NOT A PORTFOLIO PROCESS. op_16 Kelly exists but memory notes Kelly is advisory pending calibration. The 2024-2026 pattern is multi-armed bandit + fractional Kelly (0.25-0.5) with estimation risk and EXECUTION-DELAY correction (arXiv:1907.08771) — 12s block times are exactly the regime where naive HF-Kelly degrades. The ergodic growth-rate criterion (arXiv:2606.21769 Prop 1) would let per-strategy controls be wealth-independent, collapsing the 264-strategy allocation problem to per-strategy growth estimates + one global fraction — a directly implementable simplification of the 8,184-relation matrix.

10. NO FORMAL VERIFICATION OF THE MATH LAYER. Discovery equations and gates are prose/TS/Rhai; the frontier machine-checks MEV bounds and strategy optimality in Lean (arXiv:2510.14480). Even a partial port (marginal prefilter conditions, closed-route optimality) would harden the hot-path.

11. LATENCY/BUILDER ECONOMICS NOT MODELED. Builders pay ~$14M/month for first-quartile placement (arXiv:2508.04003) and searcher-builder vertical integration drives profitability (arXiv:2507.13023). The system's CEX-DEX family (14 strategies) has no builder-integration, preconfirmation, or inclusion-position dimension — on surfaces where competition is 11 incumbents, being non-integrated means being priced out regardless of detector quality.

12. NO HONEST COMPETITION/OCCUPANCY PRIOR. The stylized-facts literature (arXiv:2510.22834) shows the competitor footprint is measurable in pool statistics (JIT LPs, sandwich attackers distort cross-autocorrelations). Nothing in the 31 operators estimates "someone else is already on this pool/block" — yet the $233.8M/19-searchers data says competition intensity is the primary determinant of realized share.

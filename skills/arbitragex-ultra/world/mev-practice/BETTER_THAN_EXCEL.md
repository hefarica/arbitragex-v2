# What the world does better than our Excel

Ground truth for this comparison: I inspected the local 264-strategy catalog (c:\Users\HFRC\Desktop\arbitragex-v2-main (17)\docs\excel_strategies_extracted.json — 264 strategies, families: 36 same-chain DEX spot arb, 31 tx/state-triggered, 31 parity/redemption, 30 cross-chain, 30 derivatives/vol, 25 lending/liquidations, 20 intents/solvers/auctions, 18 NFT, 17 AMM-curve, 14 CEX-DEX, 12 prediction markets; each with Discovery_Equation prose, Primary/Secondary ops from the fixed 31-operator set) and the 31 operators (docs/excel_operators_extracted.json + CLAUDE.md table: SVD, PCA, eigen, Von Neumann entropy, PDMP, Markov, HMM, Kalman, Lévy, Welford, Bayes, MLE, regression, KL, golden-section, Kelly, Pontryagin, Lagrangian, simplex, GD, Newton, MC, M/G/1 queueing, Nash, bundle reconstruction, flash-loan, path ordering, JIT liquidation, Shapley, GNN, PPO). What the world does BETTER than this Excel:

1. JOINT OPTIMIZATION OVER A TAXONOMY OF DETECTORS. The catalog treats each strategy as an independent detector with its own discovery equation. State of the art treats the whole venue graph as ONE convex program: marginal-price root-finding with 1 variable per token, 200x faster than generic solvers (arXiv:2502.08258), with gas-aware full-venue optimum (G-FVO) as the benchmark (arXiv:2607.20762). The measured prize is 2.02 bps average shortfall on a single pair ($24M on 2.98M swaps) — most of it from STALE STATE, not from missing strategy SKUs. 264 detectors cannot express the optimal SPLIT of one trade across pools; one convex program does, and its optimality is certified.

2. LVR / ADVERSE SELECTION IS ABSENT FROM THE 31 OPERATORS. The single most consequential 2024-2026 quantity in DeFi execution math — dLVR/dt = σ²V/8, the no-arbitrage band A(f;v) = (v/8)/(1+√(2λ/v)f), per-block corrections (arXiv:2505.05113), ergodic optimal fees (arXiv:2606.21769) — has no operator. The Excel's net-profit gates model impact as static slippage; the field models it as STATE STALENESS with closed-form triggers. Every CEX-DEX/arb edge should be computed as dislocation minus an LVR-style band, not minus a slippage cap.

3. TIME/BLOCK STRUCTURE AS FIRST-CLASS MATH. Excel equations are block-agnostic. State of the art: per-block LVR closed forms, proof constant block intervals minimize LVR (arXiv:2505.05113), 20-70% LVR reduction at 100ms blocks (arXiv:2404.05803), block-time-aware execution delay breaking Kelly HF trading (arXiv:1907.08771), builders paying ~$14M/month for quartile-1 placement (arXiv:2508.04003). The Excel has no operator that consumes block-time or inclusion-position as an input.

4. REPEATED-AUCTION GAME THEORY vs STATIC NASH. The Excel has op_24 Nash equilibrium and op_29 Shapley — static, one-shot, no learning. Production MEV is a repeated first/second-price auction against ~11 incumbents with AFFILIATED valuations (arXiv:2603.16333), exclusive searcher-builder vertical integration (arXiv:2507.13023), and defection risk (arXiv:2605.22667). The winning published policy is PPO over bribe fraction with variance-shaped reward, reaching 80.93% max-profit capture vs 56.54% incumbent (arXiv:2510.14642). None of the 31 operators learn an opponent model.

5. FORMAL VERIFICATION OF STRATEGY MATH. Excel Discovery_Equations are unchecked prose. The frontier machine-checks MEV upper bounds and sandwich optimality in Lean (arXiv:2510.14480). At minimum, the marginal-prefilter Σₑ[−ln((1−fee)·rate)] < 0 and closed-route optimality conditions are exactly the class of statements now being certified.

6. EMPIRICAL ATTRUTION/REPLAY LOOPS. Production teams measure themselves against computed optima: SCO/FVO/G-FVO shortfall decomposition (arXiv:2607.20762), replayed-trade benchmarks (RediSwap beats UniswapX on 89% of replays, arXiv:2410.18434), DiD on fee switches (arXiv:2607.08525), concentration audits ($132B/11 searchers, arXiv:2401.01622; $233.8M/19 searchers, arXiv:2507.13023). The Excel catalog has readiness scores but no shortfall-accounting operator.

7. MICROSTRUCTURE DATA TRUTH. On-chain VPIN needs TRUE aggressor labels — inferred VPIN from classifiers diverges substantially from ground truth (arXiv:2606.04217, 1.2B trades). The Excel assumes detectors see clean flow; the field now quantifies classifier error as a first-class input to toxicity filters. Similarly, most flagged L2 sandwiches are false positives (arXiv:2601.19570) — precision, not recall, is the binding constraint.

8. DERIVATIVES/VOL IDENTITIES. 30 derivative strategies exist in the catalog, but the 2025-2026 identities — LVR = perpetual continuous-installment ATM option funding (arXiv:2508.02971), IL = vanilla-option strip via Carr-Madan in price/liquidity coordinates (arXiv:2603.01344), fee-implied vol + fee swaps with proven non-identifiability limits (arXiv:2509.23222, 2608.13340) — give exact replication/hedging math the catalog lacks.

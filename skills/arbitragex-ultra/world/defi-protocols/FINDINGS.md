# DeFi protocol architecture and new opportunity surfaces (2024-2026): AMM evolution (Uniswap v4, Balancer, Curve), hybrid/intent DEXs (Hyperliquid, CoW/UniswapX/Fusion, RFQ), lending (Aave V4, Morpho, Fluid), perps (dYdX v4, GMX v2), synthetic stablecoins (Ethena), restaking/LRT economics, and parallel-EVM/Move-VM chains.

## Findings

## 1. AMM EVOLUTION — UNISWAP V4 AND BEYOND

**Uniswap v4** (mainnet Jan 30, 2025 — [official launch blog](https://blog.uniswap.org/uniswap-v4-is-here); [Three Sigma explainer](https://threesigma.xyz/blog/defi/uniswap-v4-features-dynamic-fees-hooks-gas-saving); [official architecture docs](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/concepts/architecture)):
- **Singleton `PoolManager`**: all pools in ONE contract (v1-v3 = one contract per pool). Multi-hop swaps no longer transfer tokens between pool contracts — massive gas reduction on multi-pool routes (the exact topology of cyclic arbitrage). 150+ hooks existed at launch; TVL passed $1B in ~177 days; ~$700M+ daily volume by Sept 2025 ([Oak Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/analyses/innovations/uniswap-v4-new-era-for-defi), [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/protocol/uniswap-v4)).
- **Flash accounting + EIP-1153 transient storage**: during `unlock()`, all operations track only *deltas*, settled once at the end. This is structurally an arbitrage engine primitive: a multi-pool cycle pays gas for net settlement only. Flash loans inside the singleton are fee-free and cheap (ERC-6909 claims) — [MetaLamp architecture review](https://metalamp.io/magazine/article/uniswap-v4-architecture-understanding-the-basics-of-the-protocol), [Sentora deep dive](https://medium.com/sentora/inside-uniswap-v4-a-review-of-the-new-architecture-of-the-most-popular-defi-amm-346f35cf253c).
- **Hooks**: contracts called at 8 lifecycle points (before/after initialize, swap, add/removeLiquidity, donate). Killer features for opportunity generation: **dynamic fees** (fee flag governed by hook, can change per swap based on volatility/oracle deviation), **limit orders/TWAMM as hooks**, **native oracles**, JIT-liquidity defense (withdrawal locks), and **MEV-recapture hooks** (e.g. the Detox hook uses a Pyth oracle to detect toxic flow and redirect arbitrage profit to LPs — [ETHGlobal Detox](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/detox-hook-mppeg), [awesome-uniswap-hooks](https://github.com/fewwwww/awesome-uniswap-hooks)). Whole protocols now ship AS hooks: Bunni, Silo V2, EulerSwap ([DWF Labs research](https://www.dwf-labs.com/research/457-what-s-new-in-uniswap-v4-three-key-changes-and-two-new-protocols)).
- **New attack surface**: the $11M Cork Protocol exploit (May 28, 2025) was a v4-hook access-control failure — [Dedaub post-mortem](https://dedaub.com/blog/the-11m-cork-protocol-hack-a-critical-lesson-in-uniswap-v4-hook-security/), [QuillAudits hook security](https://www.quillaudits.com/research/uniswap-development/uniswap-v4/uniswap-v4-hooks-and-security). For a searcher this means: hook pools can charge *adversarial, profit-aware fees* ("MEV tax" hooks), so a static fee assumption is now a correctness bug, not just an approximation.
- **Math consequence**: the canonical prefilter Σ_e[−ln((1−fee_e)·rate_e)] < 0 (which our MEV-01-001 row uses) is only valid with static fees. In v4, fee_e = γ_e(state) — must be evaluated inside the swap simulation (hook may read the same swap's amount), i.e., detection must run the pool THROUGH the hook (revm-style simulation) instead of pure closed-form composition.

**Balancer**: extensible vault with weighted pools (invariant V = Π b_i^{w_i}, e.g. 80/20; spot rate p_i/p_j = (b_j w_i)/(b_i w_j)) and stable pools using Curve's invariant — [Balancer docs](https://docs-v2.balancer.fi/concepts/overview/basics.html), [weighted pools](https://balancer.gitbook.io/balancer-v2/products/balancer-pools/weighted-pools). Rate-provider manipulation via batch-swap sequences is a documented exploit class — [Coinspect analysis](https://www.coinspect.com/blog/balancer-rate-manipulation-exploit/) — meaning "reserve-derived price" is not always trustworthy; rate providers are oracle-like inputs on some curves.

**Curve**: StableSwap-NG supports permissionless plain pools up to 8 coins and metapools ([repo](https://github.com/curvefi/stableswap-ng), [docs](https://docs.curve.finance/developer/amm/stableswap-ng/overview), [MixBytes deep dive](https://mixbytes.io/blog/modern-dex-es-how-they-re-made-curve-stable-swap-ng)). StableSwap invariant (amplification A blends constant-sum with constant-product): A·n^n·Σx_i + D = A·D·n^n + D^(n+1)/(n^n·Πx_i). LLAMMA (crvUSD collateral AMM) implements soft-liquidation bands — a continuously-arbitraged channel between the AMM and external market prices.

## 2. NEW DEX DESIGNS — HYBRIDS, RFQ, INTENTS

**Hyperliquid** ([technical deep dive](https://rocknblock.io/blog/how-does-hyperliquid-work-a-technical-deep-dive), [Zealynx deep dive](https://www.zealynx.io/research/protocol-deep-dives/Understanding-Hyperliquid-Architecture-HyperBFT-HyperCore-HyperEVM-Part1), [A1 Research](https://a1research.io/blog/hyperliquid-the-clob-king), [OAK Research S1 2025 report](https://oakresearch.io/en/reports/protocols/hyperliquid-hype-s1-2025-activity-report)): single L1 with two execution contexts — **HyperCore** (Rust VM running a fully on-chain CLOB; no off-chain matching engine; orderbook state on-chain) and **HyperEVM** (EVM contracts sharing the same native state, with precompiles to READ exchange state). HyperBFT consensus gives sub-second finality. Opportunity surface: a smart contract can atomically react to orderbook state — CLOB↔AMM arbitrage on one finality domain, on-chain market making against the book, builder-code (HIP-3) markets.

**dYdX v4** ([docs](https://docs.dydx.xyz/concepts/architecture/overview), [technical overview](https://www.dydx.xyz/blog/v4-technical-architecture-overview)): sovereign Cosmos SDK + CometBFT appchain; validators run order placement/matching (decentralized CLOB), off-chain indexer. 267 perpetual markets and #2 perp DEX by volume as of June 2025 ([Coin Metrics](https://coinmetrics.substack.com/p/state-of-the-network-issue-318)).

**GMX v2** ([Cyfrin course](https://updraft.cyfrin.io/courses/gmx-perpetuals-trading/foundation/how-gmx-works), [gmx-synthetics repo](https://github.com/gmx-io/gmx-synthetics)): isolated per-market GM pools with oracle pricing (Chainlink low-latency feeds), GLV vault auto-rebalances liquidity across markets, single-token LP exposure (pure BTC/ETH). LP-driven model = "real yield" fee capture; pricing is oracle-based so no pool-reserve arb, but oracle-lag/Mark-price vs AMM spot spreads are the tradeable surface.

**Vertex** ([overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFKNeoeSzhU)): hybrid CLOB + AMM backstop on Arbitrum — off-chain edge matching, on-chain settlement. **Hashflow RFQ** ([Messari](https://messari.io/report/hashflow-certainty-in-execution), [Consensys](https://consensys.io/blog/hashflow-a-dex-for-bridgeless-cross-chain-swaps-zero-slippage-and-mev-protected-trades)): professional MMs sign firm quotes off-chain, settled on-chain; zero-slippage, MEV-protected, bridgeless cross-chain swaps. **0x** stitches RFQ + AMM + orderbooks in one aggregator ([Eco comparison](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15483993-rfq-vs-amm-for-institutional-stablecoin-flow), [Hummingbot primer](https://hummingbot.org/blog/exchange-types-explained-clob-rfq-amm/)).

**Intent-based**: **CoW Protocol** batches orders in ~12-30s batch auctions; solvers compete on surplus, with uniform clearing prices and coincidence-of-wants matching; ~$200M/day volume, ~63% of the intent market ([Eco explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/13064300-cow-swap-explained-mev-free-dex-trading), [Greenfield investment memo](https://greenfieldcapital.com/2024/11/04/cow/), [CoW volume comparison](https://x.com/CoWSwap/status/1904506315554373906)). **UniswapX**: Dutch auctions filled by fillers using own inventory + AMM hedges. **1inch Fusion**: resolvers compete, ~$60M/day ([BlockEden builder breakdown](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/uniswapx-cow-protocol-and-1inch-fusion-now-handle-billions-in-monthly-volume-through-solver-auctions-the-intent-architecture-explained-for-builders/462), [Gate comparison](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/is-cowswap-an-intent-based-swap-the-next-gen-dex/915)). Production pattern: arbitrage inside batches is captured BY SOLVERS and partially refunded to users as price improvement — the atomically-executable-onchain-mempool arb surface shrinks where intent flow dominates.

## 3. LENDING — AAVE V4, MORPHO, FLUID

**Aave V4** (Ethereum mainnet March 30, 2026; Avalanche July 15, 2026; $400M+ deposits by mid-2026 — [official blog](https://aave.com/blog/aave-v4-live-ethereum), [architecture post](https://aave.com/blog/understanding-aave-v4s-architecture), [docs](https://aave.com/docs/aave-v4), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/aave-v4-deposits-surpass-400m-record/)): **Hub-and-Spoke** — Liquidity Hub holds all assets; Spokes (LRT, RWA, per-asset) draw DAO-governed credit/debit lines. Unifies liquidity per chain, isolates risk in spokes, adds **risk premiums** and unified accounting ([Sentora notes](https://medium.com/sentora/some-notes-about-aave-v4-hub-spoke-architecture-risk-premiums-and-unified-accounting-0b3b8866838f), [unified liquidity thesis](https://aave.com/blog/aave-v4-unified-liquidity-thesis)). Surface: inter-spoke rate/credit-line carry; hub borrow → spoke deposit loops; eMode LST/LRT positions at 93-97% LTV ([Lido × Aave case study](https://aave.com/blog/lido-aave-case-study), [Lido Liquid eMode strategies](https://blog.lido.fi/steth-enables-new-strategies-on-aave/)).

**Morpho Blue** ([vaults explainer](https://morpho.org/blog/understanding-morpho-vaults-intro-simplifying-isolated-markets/), [Gauntlet VaultBook](https://vaultbook.gauntlet.xyz/vaults/morpho-vaults/morpho-vaults-overview), [Blockcrunch](https://blockcrunch.substack.com/p/morpho-blue-a-new-defi-bluechip-in)): immutable, governance-minimized, PERMISSIONLESS isolated markets defined by exactly (loan asset, collateral, oracle, IRM, LLTV); MetaMorpho vaults aggregate across markets. Thousands of permissionless markets = long-tail liquidation surface (liquidation bonus = LLTV/LTF spread, flash-loan the debt asset). This two-layer design (immutable core + curator vaults) is the template for Silo V2 and Euler V2 ([Logos lending ecosystem doc](https://github.com/logos-co/rfp/blob/master/appendix/lending-ecosystem.md)).

**Fluid (Instadapp)** ([docs](https://docs.fluid.instadapp.io/), [Oak Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/reports/protocols/fluid-new-defi-standard-unify-dex-lending), [Messari](https://messari.io/report/fluid-re-architecting-defi-liquidity), [MixBytes Fluid DEX](https://mixbytes.io/blog/modern-dex-es-how-they-re-made-fluid-dex)): single **Liquidity Layer** where vaults' collateral/debt simultaneously serve as DEX liquidity — deposits earn lending interest AND swap fees; up to 95% LTV; **Automated Limits** adjust debt/collateral ceilings EVERY BLOCK ([Fluid intro](https://fluid.io/blog/protocol-introducing-fluid), [Impossible Finance](https://blog.impossible.finance/everything-you-need-to-know-about-fluid/)). Surface: swap-through-collateral routes with near-zero marginal depth cost; per-block limit changes create transient dislocations.

## 4. PERPETUALS/DERIVATIVES
See Hyperliquid/dYdX/GMX above. The core new surfaces: (a) funding-rate arbitrage and cash-and-carry basis (spot vs perp), (b) oracle-priced markets vs AMM spot spreads (GMX Mark price vs Curve/Uni), (c) on-chain orderbook microstructure (Hyperliquid) where CLOB BBO is readable on-chain via HyperEVM precompiles, (d) GLV auto-rebalancing flows (predictable taker flow to front-run or provide into).

## 5. STABLECOINS — DELTA-NEUTRAL SYNTHETIC DOLLARS
**Ethena USDe** ([mechanism docs](https://docs.ethena.fi/), [Chainlink synthetic dollars](https://chain.link/synthetic-dollars), [ChainArgos risk case study](https://www.chainargos.com/), [ECO 2026 overview](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15254002-ethena-usde-and-susde-2026-delta-neutral-yield)): hold spot ETH/BTC + short equal perpetual notional → delta-neutral; yield = funding received + staking yield − fees. Documented risks: funding flips negative in bearish regimes, CEX counterparty (Binance/Bybit), off-exchange settlement custody, liquidation on sharp moves. Surface: sUSDe yield trading, funding-basis carry as a systematic strategy class, and USDe/USDT/USDC stableswap pools as low-risk parity channels. **Pendle** tokenizes this: PT (principal, fixed yield) / YT (yield-only) splits of ANY yield-bearing asset including sUSDe — yield curve arbitrage across maturities ([official](https://www.pendle.finance/), [strategy panorama](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/29529969269474), [Altrady guide](https://www.altrady.com/blog/cryptocurrency/pendle-yield-trading-pt-yt-guide-2026), [MixBytes technical](https://mixbytes.io/blog/yield-tokenization-protocols-how-they-re-made-pendle)).

## 6. RESTAKING/LRT
**EigenLayer** ([economics deep dive](https://www.cryptoeq.io/articles/eigenLayer-economics-governance), [QuickNode 2025](https://www.quicknode.com/blog/restaking-revolution-eigenlayer-defi-yields-2025), [slashing accounting](https://wag3s.io/blog/eigenlayer-restaking-accounting)): ~4.7M ETH (~$15B) restaked ([Crypto.com research](https://crypto.com/en/research/restaking-eigenlayer-ecosystem-april-2024)); slashing went live mid-2025 per-AVS. Competing framework Symbiotic ([Oak Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/analyses/innovations/restaking-war-eigenlayer-vs-symbiotic)). **LRT peg dynamics** ([Gate research](https://web02.gate.com.tr/en/learn/articles/how-liquid-are-liquid-restaking-tokens)): LRTs trade at discounts/premiums to NAV; redemption requires multi-step, DELAYED withdrawal (queue + epoch), so instant arbitrage is blocked and the discount behaves like a term basis ([arXiv yield-aggregator analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23298v1)). **LST looping** is the flagship consumer strategy: deposit wstETH → borrow WETH → swap to more wstETH → repeat, amplifying (staking yield − borrow rate); same with LRTs in Aave eMode ([Galaxy State of Onchain Yield](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/the-state-of-onchain-yield), [LlamaRisk LST pricing](https://llamarisk.com/research/lst-pricing), [Chainlink LST/LRT oracles](https://chain.link/blog/chainlink-for-lsts-and-lrts)).

## 7. NEW L1s/L2s — PARALLEL EVM AND MOVE
**Monad** ([LinkedIn parallel-execution explainer](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-monad-how-we-learned-stop-worrying-start-loving-parallel-c7ije), [Blockcrunch Sei-vs-Monad](https://blockcrunch.substack.com/p/sei-network-everything-you-need-to)): optimistic parallel execution with conflict re-execution, MonadBFT, MonadDB; parallel EVM back in top-15 trending ([Yellow.com](https://yellow.com/news/monad-trending-top-15-parallel-evm-claim-back-focus)); low-latency RPC race already started ([Dwellir Monad RPC benchmarks](https://www.dwellir.com/blog/best-monad-rpc-providers)). **Sei v2** — first parallelized EVM, ordering rules suppress sandwich MEV, pushing opportunity to backruns/arb/liquidations ([Sei blog](https://blog.sei.io/sei-v2-the-first-parallelized-evm/), [Gate comparison incl. Monad/Neon/Eclipse](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/parallel-evms-monad-sei-v2-neon-eclipse/2242)). **Sui/Aptos (Move VM)**: object model + owned-object parallelism; on Sui, ARBITRAGE AND LIQUIDATIONS DOMINATE MEV (sandwich largely infeasible) — Shio Labs reported ~$60M extracted ([Sui official MEV post](https://www.sui.io/blog/mev-on-sui-current-state), [Shio Labs thread](https://x.com/ShioLabs/status/1858885460459999345)); academic quantification of spam-MEV on Sui/Aptos ([arXiv 2604.00234](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00234v2)). Cross-chain reality check 2025: 90%+ of arbitrage routes through PRIVATE channels, not public mempools ([Extropy cross-chain MEV analysis](https://academy.extropy.io/pages/articles/mev-crosschain-analysis-2025.html)).

## CROSS-CUTTING: CEX-DEX AND PRIVATE ORDERFLOW (the actual state of the art)
- **CEX-DEX arbitrage is the dominant profit pool and a latency game**: empirical measurement of CEX-DEX extracted value and searcher competition shows competition erodes margins toward latency/infrastructure advantage ([arXiv 2507.13023](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.13023v2), [ethresear.ch empirical analysis](https://ethresear.ch/t/empirical-analysis-of-cross-domain-cex-dex-arbitrage-on-ethereum/17620), [latency-advantage talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r7hmxQ8DTA)).
- **MEV-Share**: programmably private orderflow auctions — users/wallets internalize their MEV, searchers bid for backrun rights ([Flashbots docs](https://docs.flashbots.net/flashbots-mev-share/introduction), [collective post](https://collective.flashbots.net/t/mev-share-programmably-private-orderflow-to-share-mev-with-users/1264), [repo](https://github.com/flashbots/mev-share), [searcher guide](https://writings.flashbots.net/searching-on-mev-share)). **MEV Blocker** rebates builder payments to orderflow originators; 2025 benchmark of private-RPC protection ([arXiv 2505.19708](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19708v1)). **SUAVE** unbundles mempool+builder cross-chain ([Flashbots](https://www.flashbots.net/), [SUAVE post](https://collective.flashbots.net/t/the-future-of-mev-is-suave/762)). Flashbots' "MEV and the Limits of Scaling" shows MEV spam constrains throughput economics ([writings](https://writings.flashbots.net/mev-and-the-limits-of-scaling)).

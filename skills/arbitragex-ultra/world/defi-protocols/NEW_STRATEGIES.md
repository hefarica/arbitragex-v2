# New strategies not in our 264 catalog

New strategy types NOT in the 264 catalog, with topology and math:

**S-1. Hook-adaptive v4 cycle arbitrage (DEX_AMM family extension).** Topology: closed cycle over mixed v3/v4/hook pools inside one v4 `unlock()`; net-delta flash accounting settlement; ERC-6909 fee-free flash. Math: same cycle product Q_R(x)=q_n(...q_1(x)) but each v4 leg's fee γ_e(x, s_e) is a function of swap size and hook state, so optimum must be found by simulation-embedded root-finding (bisect/Newton on x with revm hook execution), not closed-form. Prefilter becomes stochastic: run min-size probe swap to observe effective fee, then decide. New detector: enumerate pools by hook flags BEFORE_SWAP|AFTER_SWAP and route hook pools only through the simulator path. Cap: profit conditional on hook not invoking MEV-tax — model fee as γ = γ0 + κ·max(0, oracleDeviation) and require Π_R(x) > 0 at observed κ.

**S-2. Solver-side batch surplus capture (INTENT_AUCTION made executable).** Topology: subscribe to CoW/UniswapX/Fusion orderflow; solve batch: maximize Σ_u (v_u(p_exec) − v_u(p_limit)) over uniform clearing prices p per token-pair, s.t. solvency against own inventory + on-chain AMM hedges executed in settlement. Coincidence-of-wants matching (user A sells X for Y, user B sells Y for X) creates surplus with ZERO market impact — value invisible to any route-graph detector. Math: LP-style feasibility — find p minimizing inventory turnover Σ|ΔI_i(p)| with all limit prices satisfied; UniswapX variant: Dutch auction decay d(t) means filler profit shrinks linearly until it crosses AMM-hedge cost — optimal fill time t* = argmax (spread(t) − hedgeCost(t)). Requires a NEW execution primitive: inventory ledger + non-atomic unwind (the Excel's NonAtomic_Type, never implemented).

**S-3. Funding-basis carry with Pendle fixed-yield hedge (DERIVATIVES × PARITY_REDEMPTION).** Topology: long spot ETH (or staked yETH) + short perp on Hyperliquid/GMX (collect funding when positive); hedge funding-flip risk by buying PT on sUSDe/PT-eETH to lock fixed yield on the yield leg. Math: net carry = (E[funding] + y_stake) − (borrow + fees); variance of the funding leg swapped for fixed via PT at implied rate y_fixed = (FV/P_PT)^(1/T) − 1. Entry condition: E[funding] (EWMA over 30d) − y_fixed > threshold; exit on funding EMA crossover. Inventory-based, per-block funding accrual accounting — a new opportunity TYPE (carry, not dislocation).

**S-4. Hyperliquid CLOB ↔ AMM atomic arbitrage via HyperEVM precompiles.** Topology: HyperEVM contract reads CLOB best-bid/offer + depth through exchange precompiles; if |CLOB_mid − AMM_price|·q > fees + slippage(q), atomically take AMM pool and post/execute opposing CLOB order within the same sub-second block window. Math: standard two-venue arb with explicit depth-limited size q* = argmax |p_CLOB(q) − p_AMM(q)|·q − c(q), where p_CLOB(q) walks the on-chain book. Uniquely possible because both venues share one finality domain — no bridge/trust gap. This is the first venue pair where orderbook-vs-AMM is atomic.

**S-5. Morpho/Fluid long-tail liquidation sniper (LENDING family extension).** Topology: event-driven index of ALL permissionless Morpho markets (market-created events) + Fluid vaults; monitor health factor HF = collateral·price·LTF / debt < 1 per block; on breach, flash-loan debt asset, repay, seize collateral, swap, repay flash. Math: profit = bonus·debt·(collateral_value/debt − LLTV-adjusted terms) − gas − swap slippage; with graduated LTF, deeper underwater = bigger bonus → optimal threshold solving ∂E[profit]/∂HF-trigger = 0 under liquidation-competition intensity λ(HF) (hazard model from historical competition data). Fluid twist: per-block Automated Limits change ceilings, creating sudden near-liquidation clusters — a state-trigger (our DEX_STATE family's "state-transition arb") applied to lending.

**S-6. LRT discount/redemption term-basis arbitrage (PARITY_REDEMPTION with queue modeling).** Topology: when P_LRT < NAV·(1−θ), buy LRT on Curve StableSwap-NG/Uni, either (a) redeem through delayed withdrawal queue (term = queue delay T), or (b) hold and sell when P→NAV. Math: expected annualized return = (NAV−P)/(P·T) − fees − risk premium; the discount is effectively a futures basis with delivery T — trade it against Pendle PT yields of matching maturity for a term-structure arb (buy cheap LRT discount, short equivalent-duration fixed yield). Inventory-based, requires redemption-queue telemetry (new data binding: queue length, epoch timing, AVS slashing state).

**S-7. Aave V4 inter-spoke credit-line carry.** Topology: borrow asset from Liquidity Hub at hub rate r_h; deposit into spoke offering risk-premium r_s > r_h against accepted collateral; loop within DAO credit-line cap C. Math: carry = (r_s − r_h)·L·equity bounded by min(C, LTV constraint); monitor governance votes changing caps (a state-trigger on DAO events, not on-chain price) — pre-V4 this surface literally did not exist because liquidity was siloed per pool.

**S-8. MEV-Share rebate-aware backrunning.** Topology: subscribe to MEV-Share hints (user swap visible pre-execution with programmable privacy); simulate backrun b after user tx u on affected pools; if profit(b) > bid floor, submit bundle [u, b] with rebate split to user. Math: bid strategy = maximize profit − rebate − gas s.t. rebate high enough to win the OFA auction vs other searchers (first-price-ish); conversion of public-mempool competition into an auction where the searcher's edge is simulation speed, not spam. Direct upgrade path for our existing backrun detectors with lower cost basis than public competition.

**S-9. Sui/Move concurrent arb+liquidation engine (non-EVM expansion).** Topology: owned-object parallelism lets one bot fire hundreds of non-conflicting txs concurrently (different pools/positions); cyclic arb across Cetus/Navi/FlowX + liquidations on Navi/Scallop. Math: same cycle product detection but contention-aware scheduling — maximize expected profit subject to shared-object write conflicts (parallel scheduling problem, M/G/1-queue delay modeling from our 31-operator arsenal applies directly). Sandwich mostly infeasible on Sui (ordering), so the entire edge reallocates to arb+liquidation speed — an efficiency frontier shift, not a new formula.

**S-10. Dynamic-fee volatility harvest (v4 hook pools).** Topology: v4 pools whose hooks raise fees on volatility/oracle deviation systematically over-reward LPs or under-charge calm periods; strategy = route flow through the pool when effective fee < fair fee given current realized vol (taker side) and provide JIT liquidity when effective fee > fair (LP side, one-block positions using beforeSwap/afterSwap hooks). Math: fair fee = c·σ_realized·√(impact); edge = γ_hook − fair; LP leg is the modern JIT-MEV strategy executed THROUGH hooks rather than against them.

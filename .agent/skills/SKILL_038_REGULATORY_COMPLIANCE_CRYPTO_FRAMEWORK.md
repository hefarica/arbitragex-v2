## TAX COMPLIANCE
```
- Selling crypto for fiat: Capital gain/loss
- Trading crypto for crypto: Taxable event
- Mining/staking: Ordinary income
- Cost basis: FIFO (default), LIFO, HIFO, Specific ID
- Reporting: Form 8949, Schedule D, FBAR, Form 8938
```
